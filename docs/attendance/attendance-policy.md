## 1. Overview & Attendance Calculation

At VIT-AP University, attendance is monitored continuously for each registered theory, laboratory, and embedded course under the Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS).

* **Calculation Period:** Attendance calculation begins from the date of course registration / commencement of classes for the semester up to the last instructional day before examinations.
* **Tracking Mechanism:** Attendance is recorded by course faculty per lecture/lab session and is synchronized with the **VTOP Student Portal**.
* **Student Responsibility:** Students are responsible for monitoring their subject-wise attendance percentage directly on VTOP on a weekly basis.

## 2. Minimum Attendance Threshold & Exam Eligibility

### Standard Requirement
* To be eligible to appear for the **Final Assessment Test (FAT)**, students must maintain regular attendance across all enrolled courses.
* The widely cited benchmark in VIT university regulations is a minimum of **75% attendance** per registered course.

## 3. Condonation & Medical Leave Procedures

### Medical Condonation
* Students who fall short of the standard threshold due to documented illness, hospitalization, or genuine medical emergencies may apply for **Medical Condonation**.
* **Submission Timeline:** Medical certificates and discharge summaries must typically be submitted to the student's Proctor and the School Dean/Academic Office within a specified number of working days following the student's return to campus.
* **Verification Note:** A medical certificate does not automatically guarantee attendance credit; condonation is subject to review and approval by the Medical Officer and Academic Review Committee.

### On-Duty (OD) Attendance
* **Eligibility:** Granted to students representing VIT-AP University in authorized academic conferences, co-curricular events, sports competitions, or placement recruitment drives.
* **Approval Flow:** OD forms must be endorsed by the organizing faculty/club coordinator and sanctioned by the respective Head of Department (HOD) or Director of Student Welfare prior to the event.

## 4. Discrepancy Rectification & Proctorship

* **Reporting Period:** If a student notices an error in their attendance recorded on VTOP (e.g., marked absent when present), the student must approach the concerned course faculty member immediately.
* **Proctor Consultation:** Every student is assigned a faculty **Proctor** who acts as an academic mentor. Students encountering persistent attendance difficulties or health issues must keep their Proctor informed.
