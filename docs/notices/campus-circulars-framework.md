## 1. Official Communication Channels (Verified)

To protect students from unverified rumors and informal social media misinformation, VIT-AP University recognizes only the following authoritative channels for official announcements:

1. **VTOP Digital Notice Board:** The primary portal where authenticated circulars regarding academic calendars, timetable releases, examination schedules, fee payment deadlines, and hostel announcements are published.
2. **Official Student Email (`@vitap.ac.in`):** Direct electronic notices issued by university authorities (Registrar, Deans, Directors) delivered to students' individual institutional inboxes.
3. **Dean of Academics Office:** Issues all authoritative memos regarding instructional days, continuous assessment calendars, FFCS schedules, and grading announcements.
4. **Directorate of Student Welfare (DSW):** Issues circulars regarding student clubs, campus cultural events, sports meets, transport schedules, and health advisories.
5. **Office of the Registrar:** Issues statutory university declarations, public holiday schedules, and general institutional administrative orders.
