## 1. Structure of Academic Assessments

Under the credit system at VIT-AP, student academic evaluation is divided into **Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT)** and a **Final Assessment Test (FAT)**.

* **Continuous Assessment:** Comprises internal written tests, online/offline quizzes, digital assignments, laboratory continuous evaluations, and project reviews conducted during the semester.
  * **CAT-1:** First Continuous Assessment Test covering the initial modules.
  * **CAT-2:** Second Continuous Assessment Test covering subsequent instructional units.
* **Final Assessment Test (FAT):** Comprehensive final examination conducted at the conclusion of the semester instructional period.

---

## 2. Examination Eligibility & Hall Tickets

* **Hall Ticket Issuance:** Students must download their digital Hall Ticket / Examination Schedule via the **VTOP Portal** prior to the exam series.
* **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Valid VIT-AP Physical Student ID Card.
  2. Printed/digital Hall Ticket showing confirmed seat allotment and room number.
  3. Adherence to mandatory attendance thresholds across course components.
* **Disqualification / Debarment:** Students who have been debarred due to attendance shortfall or disciplinary sanctions are not eligible to receive hall tickets for the affected courses.

---

## 3. Grading System & Performance Indicators

VIT-AP employs a Letter Grading system reflecting relative or absolute student performance:

* **Grade Point Scale (Standard 10-Point Framework):**
  * **S Grade:** Outstanding performance (Grade Point: 10)
  * **A Grade:** Excellent performance (Grade Point: 9)
  * **B Grade:** Very Good (Grade Point: 8)
  * **C Grade:** Good (Grade Point: 7)
  * **D Grade:** Average (Grade Point: 6)
  * **E Grade:** Satisfactory / Pass (Grade Point: 5)
  * **F Grade:** Fail (Grade Point: 0)
  * **N Grade:** Debarred due to lack of attendance (Grade Point: 0)

## 4. Re-evaluation & Grade Review Procedure

* **Review of Answer Scripts:** Following FAT result announcements on VTOP, a designated window is opened where students may request to view evaluated answer scripts.
* **Re-evaluation Request:** Students who identify evaluation discrepancies may formally submit a Re-evaluation application via VTOP within the prescribed deadline, subject to the standard administrative processing fee.
* **Outcome Publication:** Revised marks and grades are updated directly on the student’s VTOP grade card following re-assessment by an external or senior faculty examiner.

---

## 5. Make-up / Arrear Examination Guidelines

* **Make-up Exams:** Permitted exclusively for students who were unable to appear for scheduled exams due to authorized medical hospitalization or genuine compassionate grounds approved by the Dean of Academics.
* **Arrear Registrations:** Students earning an 'F' grade must re-register for the course in subsequent semesters or through Weekend / Summer semester offerings according to FFCS rules.
