## 2. Semester Tuition Payment Workflows

* **Payment Gateway:** All tuition and semester academic fees are paid digitally through the secure payment gateway on the VTOP portal (supporting Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards, and UPI).
* **Payment Receipts:** Automated fee receipts with reference transaction numbers are generated on VTOP immediately following successful bank clearance. Students must download and archive these receipts.
* **Late Payment Policy:** Deadlines for semester fee clearance are published in the Academic Calendar. Payment beyond the specified due date incurs late payment fines as determined by university accounts.

## 3. Tuition Fee Refund Guidelines (Verified)

VIT-AP University complies with the regulatory notifications issued by the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** and the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)** regarding admission cancellations and fee refunds.

* **Application Procedure:** Students seeking withdrawal must initiate a formal withdrawal request online through the admissions/VTOP student portal.
* **Processing Timeline:** Following administrative approval and verification of no-dues status, refund disbursements are processed and credited to the original remitting account within **15 to 30 working days**.
* **Deduction Schedule:** Proportionate deductions apply depending on whether the withdrawal request is lodged prior to or after the formal commencement of classes, in strict adherence to current UGC directives.

---

## 4. Hostel Fee Refund Policy (Verified)

The allocation and refund of hostel accommodation fees adhere to the following institutional principles:

* **Admission Fee:** The one-time hostel admission fee is strictly **non-refundable**, even if the student does not take physical possession of the allotted room.
* **Withdrawal Prior to Commencement:** If a withdrawal request is submitted before the formal commencement of the academic program, the hostel fee (excluding the admission fee) is refunded in full.
* **Withdrawal Post Commencement (Within 4 Months):** If a student vacates within 4 months of program commencement, **10% of the total hostel fee is deducted for every month of stay** (a fraction of a month is computed as a full calendar month), and the residual balance is refunded.
* **Withdrawal Post 4 Months:** Refunds are determined by pro-rata calculations for the unexpired months and mess bill adjustments approved by the Chief Warden.
