## 1. Residential Infrastructure & Amenities (Verified)

VIT-AP University maintains residential hostel blocks on campus designed to ensure a secure, hygienic living environment.

* **Room Types:** Both **Air-Conditioned (AC)** and **Non-AC** room configurations are available in various occupancy options (ranging from 2-bed to multi-bed rooms).
* **Furnishings:** Each allocated resident is provided with a standard cot, study desk, chair, and personal wardrobe/cupboard.
* **Campus Amenities:**
  * **Network:** High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity throughout residential blocks.
  * **Academic Study:** Dedicated reading halls and a **Night Library** accessible for hostellers.
  * **Health & Essentials:** On-campus Health Center with medical assistance, ambulance service, daily provisions store, and night cafeteria facilities.

---

## 2. Safety, Surveillance & Biometric Protocol (Verified)

* **Physical Security:** 24/7 manned security checkpoints at all hostel gates and access points.
* **Biometric Tracking:** Biometric registration systems and CCTV surveillance monitor entries and exits to residential blocks.
* **Wardens:** Resident Wardens reside on-site to provide immediate administrative and emergency assistance around the clock.

---

## 3. Catering & Mess Management (Verified)

* **Mess Types:** Hostellers select from designated mess categories:
  * **Vegetarian Mess:** Balanced vegetarian nutrition.
  * **Non-Vegetarian Mess:** Regular non-vegetarian menu inclusions.
  * **Special Mess:** Extended culinary menu options.
* **Student Mess Committee:** The mess menu is evaluated and updated on a monthly basis through the joint **Student Mess and Menu Committee** collaborating with hostel administrators.
* **Mess Transfer:** Students have designated windows at the start of terms to request mess transfers through the hostel office, subject to capacity.

---

## 4. Key Residential Rules & Conduct (Verified & Pending Rules)

* **Cooking Prohibition (Verified):** Cooking or operating electrical heating appliances inside hostel rooms is strictly prohibited under fire-safety regulations.
* **Evening Attendance (Verified):** Mandatory evening biometric / roll-call attendance is recorded daily in all blocks.
* **Prohibited Substances (Verified):** Possession or consumption of tobacco, alcohol, or illicit substances is strictly prohibited on university property and leads to immediate expulsion and disciplinary action.
