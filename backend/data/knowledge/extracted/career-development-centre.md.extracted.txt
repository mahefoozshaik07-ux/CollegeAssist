> **Target Audience:** Pre-Final & Final Year Students

---

## 1. Role of the Career Development Centre (CDC) (Verified)

The **Career Development Centre (CDC)** at VIT-AP University directs all training, internship placements, and campus recruitment processes, operating within the unified centralized placement network of the VIT group.

* **Industry Readiness Training:** CDC coordinates structured preparation courses, including quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, coding assessments, group discussions, and technical/HR mock interviews.
* **Internship Facilitation:** CDC coordinates mandatory summer and semester-long industrial internships that frequently translate into full-time Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).
* **Corporate Connect:** Hosts pre-placement talks (PPTs), technical hackathons, guest lectures, and industry leadership summits throughout the academic year.

---

## 2. Placement Offer Classification Framework (Verified)

Campus recruitment opportunities are categorized based on compensation and job role profiles:

1. **Super Dream Offers:** Premium compensation tier featuring elite technology, quantitative finance, and product engineering roles (e.g., packages exceeding standard benchmark ceilings, with top offers reaching ₹90+ LPA).
2. **Dream Offers:** Core engineering and leading technology firm placements offering high compensation packages.
3. **Regular / IT Services Offers:** Large-scale corporate recruitment drives offering industry-standard software engineering and graduate trainee positions.

---

## 3. Prominent Recruiting Partners (Verified)

VIT-AP students are recruited by over 800+ multinational corporations and core engineering enterprises, including:

* **Software & Technology:** Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Infosys, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), Cognizant, Wipro, Accenture.
* **Core Engineering & Semiconductors:** Intel, Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, Bosch, Schneider Electric, Larsen & Toubro (L&T), Tata Motors.
* **Consulting, Analytics & Finance:** Deloitte, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan Chase & Co.

---

## 4. Student Registration & Eligibility Protocols

* **CDC Portal Registration:** Eligible students must formally register on the CDC placement portal during the pre-final year and maintain an up-to-date academic record, resume, and contact details.
* **Attendance at Recruitment Drives:** Once a student registers for a company drive, attendance at the company’s pre-placement talk, online assessment, and subsequent interview rounds is mandatory. Unexcused absence leads to temporary placement deactivation.
