## 1. Zero Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy (Verified)

VIT-AP University strictly enforces the **UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions**. Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, psychological, or electronic) inside or outside campus is a cognizable criminal offense.

### Official Reporting Channels
* **Anti-Ragging Committee Email:** `anti.ragging@vitap.ac.in`
* **Anti-Ragging Direct Hotline:** `7901059255`
* **National Anti-Ragging Helpline:** `1800-180-5522` (Toll-Free, 24x7)
* **Action & Penalties:** Any complaint received is submitted to the University Anti-Ragging Committee and forwarded to the Vice-Chancellor. Penalties include suspension, rustication, forfeiture of scholarship, and police FIR registration.

## 2. University Disciplinary Committee (UDC) & Student Ethics (Verified)

* **Governance:** The University Disciplinary Committee (UDC) is comprised of senior university faculty, school deans, and administrative heads responsible for maintaining discipline across academic blocks, laboratories, and hostel zones.
* **Student Code of Ethics:** Students must demonstrate honesty in academic work, respect campus property, and maintain decorum. Malpractice in examinations, laboratory falsification, or unauthorized access to campus IT infrastructure results in immediate UDC summons.
* **Campus ID Card Protocol:** Students are required to wear their official VIT-AP ID card visibly at all times while present in academic blocks, libraries, and administrative offices. Security and faculty have the authority to inspect ID cards at any time.

## 3. Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS) Principles (Verified)

The Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS) empowers students to construct their own individualized academic roadmap.

* **Self-Paced Learning:** Students can balance their academic workload across semesters according to their learning pace and career goals.
* **Registration Capabilities via VTOP:**
  1. Selection of courses (Core, Program Electives, Open Electives).
  2. Choice of course instructors / faculty members.
  3. Choice of class schedule time slots (Morning vs Afternoon theory slots) to optimize study and project time.
* **Add/Drop Window:** At the beginning of each semester, a designated Add/Drop period on VTOP allows students to adjust course registrations subject to seat availability and prerequisite fulfillment.

## 4. Campus IT, Lab & Cyber Security Regulations

* **Network Access:** Campus Wi-Fi and computing labs are provided for educational and research pursuits.
* **Prohibited Activities:** Attempting to bypass proxy configurations, access restricted domains, or engage in peer-to-peer downloading of copyrighted material is strictly prohibited.
* **Digital Identity:** Student VTOP credentials and student email accounts (`@vitap.ac.in`) are personal and confidential. Sharing login credentials is a punishable offense under university IT safety rules.
